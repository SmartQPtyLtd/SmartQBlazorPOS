## How dual licensing works (and why it's possible)

SmartQ (Pty) Ltd is the sole copyright holder of this codebase. As the copyright
holder, SmartQ may offer the identical code under multiple licenses
simultaneously. Your obligations depend solely on which license you received the
code under:

- Received it from this public repository? → AGPL-3.0 applies.
- Received it under a signed commercial agreement with SmartQ? → Your
  commercial terms apply.

Choosing the AGPL route never converts your copy into a commercially licensed
one, and vice versa.
